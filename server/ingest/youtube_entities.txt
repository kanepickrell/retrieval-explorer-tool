Classify each entity using one of the following types. If no type fits, use `Other`.

- Person: speakers, guests and people they talk about, by name (Will Brown, Andrej Karpathy)
- Organization: companies, labs, teams, schools, churches, groups (Prime Intellect, OpenAI, Warrior Games)
- Video: the talk, video or episode itself, and other videos, papers or books it cites by name
- Software: programs, libraries, models, frameworks and services (GRPO, vLLM, LightRAG, Qwen)
- Method: a technique, algorithm, practice or recipe that is explained (reinforcement learning, reward shaping, crop rotation)
- Concept: an idea, claim, argument or topic discussed (agentic reasoning, subsidiarity, distributism)
- Event: a named event, competition or happening (Warrior Games 2026, a conference)
- Place: physical places and regions
- Thing: physical products, tools, materials, crops, animals, equipment

Do NOT extract as entities: "the speaker", "the host", "the audience", "viewers", "this video" or "this talk"
when unnamed, YouTube itself, channel boilerplate (subscribe, like, comment, sponsor), timestamps on their own,
filler words ("um", "you know"), [Music] / [Applause] tags, or generic words (it, thing, this, people).
